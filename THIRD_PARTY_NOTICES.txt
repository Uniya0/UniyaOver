FFmpeg (LGPL)
-------------
This product dynamically links against FFmpeg shared libraries (avcodec,
avformat, avutil, swresample, swscale) from a BtbN win64 lgpl-shared build.

FFmpeg is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPL) v2.1+
/ LGPL v3 as shipped with the binary package. Full text is in:

  third_party/ffmpeg/LICENSE.txt
  (also copied next to UniyaOver as FFMPEG-LICENSE.txt)

Source for the FFmpeg binaries used here:
  https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds

You can replace the DLLs with another compatible LGPL FFmpeg shared build.
